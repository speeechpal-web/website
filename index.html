FAQ

Questions people ask before they start.

Short answers about practice, platforms, and how SpeechPal fits next to other apps.

What is SpeechPal?

SpeechPal is a social confidence coach. You practice real conversations out loud — small talk, groups, dates, and hard talks — until freeze turns into a first line.

Who is SpeechPal for?

Anyone who knows what they want to say until the moment arrives: joining a group, first dates, networking, interviews, or everyday small talk.

How much time do I need each day?

A few minutes. SpeechPal is built around short daily reps: openings, follow-ups, and roleplay for the situations that actually freeze you.

How is this different from reading tips?

Tips stay in your head. SpeechPal makes you say the line out loud, get feedback, and repeat until it feels like you.

How is SpeechPal different from Gleam, ELSA, or public-speaking apps?

Specialists win on specialty. Gleam is EQ lessons. ELSA is pronunciation. Orai, Speeko, and Yoodli lean public speaking or enterprise roleplay. SpeechPal is spoken practice for real social moments.

Is SpeechPal on Android?

SpeechPal is on the App Store for iPhone now. Google Play is coming soon.

Do I need to already be outgoing?

No. Social confidence is a skill, not a personality trait. You start with one first line and build from there.

How do I get started?

Download SpeechPal on the App Store and start with today’s plan. A few minutes is enough to begin.
